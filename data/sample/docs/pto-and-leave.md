# Paid Time Off and Leave

Acme Cloud wants people to rest. Time off is planned with your manager and recorded in the HR system so that teams can plan coverage.

## Vacation

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid vacation per year, accrued monthly. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over into the first quarter of the next year; carried-over days that are not used by March 31 expire. Vacation of more than ten consecutive working days should be requested at least four weeks in advance.

In addition, the whole company closes for a winter break between December 24 and January 1. These days do not count against personal vacation.

## Public holidays

Employees observe the public holidays of the country they work in. If a public holiday falls on a weekend, the following Monday is taken off where local law allows.

## Sick leave

Employees can take up to 10 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is only required for absences longer than three consecutive working days. Sick leave can also be used to care for a sick child or partner.

## Parental leave

Birthing parents receive 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Non-birthing parents, including adoptive and foster parents, receive 16 weeks of fully paid leave. Parental leave can be taken in up to two blocks within the first 12 months after the birth or placement. Employees are eligible after three months of employment.

## Volunteering and other leave

Every employee gets two paid volunteering days per year for work with a registered charity. Bereavement leave is five paid days for the loss of an immediate family member and two days for other relatives. Jury duty is fully paid for as long as it lasts.

## Sabbatical

After five years of continuous employment, employees can take a four-week paid sabbatical. The sabbatical must be taken within the following two years and can be combined with regular vacation.
