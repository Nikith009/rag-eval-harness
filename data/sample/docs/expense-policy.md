# Expense Policy

This policy explains what Acme Cloud pays for and how to get reimbursed. The guiding rule is simple: spend company money as you would spend your own, and only for business purposes.

## Submitting expenses

All expenses are submitted through the expense tool with an itemized receipt. Receipts are required for every expense above 25 USD. Expenses must be submitted within 60 days of the purchase; claims older than 60 days are rejected unless the finance team grants an exception. Approved expenses are reimbursed in the next payroll cycle, which runs on the 15th and the last business day of each month.

## Approval limits

Expenses up to 500 USD are approved by the employee's direct manager. Expenses between 500 and 5,000 USD also need approval from the department head. Anything above 5,000 USD, including software contracts, requires sign-off from the finance team before the purchase is made. Splitting a purchase into smaller expenses to stay under an approval limit is a policy violation.

## Travel

Book flights and hotels through the company travel tool. Economy class is the default for flights under six hours; premium economy is allowed for flights of six hours or longer. Hotels should cost no more than 250 USD per night in most cities and 350 USD per night in high-cost cities such as New York, London, San Francisco and Zurich. When traveling for work, employees receive a daily meal allowance of 75 USD.

## Software and subscriptions

Before buying any software, check the approved tools list. New software that will handle customer data must pass a security review, which takes about five business days. Personal subscriptions to AI assistants or developer tools can be expensed up to 40 USD per month if the employee's manager approves them.

## Learning budget

Every employee has a learning budget of 1,500 USD per calendar year for courses, books, certifications and conference tickets. Unused learning budget does not roll over to the next year. Conference travel paid from the learning budget follows the travel rules above.

## Not reimbursable

Alcohol is not reimbursable except at approved team events. Fines, including parking and speeding tickets, are never reimbursed. Upgrades such as seat selection fees or lounge access are not covered unless a medical need is documented with People Operations.
