# Remote Work Policy

Acme Cloud is a remote-first company. Employees may work from anywhere in the countries where we have a registered entity: the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom and Germany. Working from another country for more than 30 consecutive days requires written approval from People Operations, because it can create tax and payroll obligations for the company.

## Core hours

Every team agrees on a four-hour block of core collaboration hours. For teams spread across North America and Europe, the default block is 9:00 to 13:00 US Central Time. Outside core hours, employees choose their own schedule. Managers should not schedule recurring meetings outside a team's core hours without the agreement of everyone invited.

## Home office stipend

New employees receive a one-time home office stipend of 1,200 USD (or the local equivalent) to buy a desk, chair, monitor and other equipment. After the first year, employees receive an annual refresh allowance of 300 USD. Equipment bought with the stipend belongs to the employee. Company laptops remain company property and must be returned within 14 days of leaving Acme Cloud.

## Internet and coworking

The company reimburses up to 60 USD per month for home internet. Employees who prefer a coworking space can expense a membership of up to 250 USD per month instead of the internet reimbursement; the two cannot be combined in the same month.

## In-person time

Each team meets in person twice a year for an offsite of three to four days. Travel and lodging for offsites are booked through the company travel tool and do not count against personal budgets. The whole company gathers once a year for the annual summit, usually held in September.

## Security expectations

Remote work does not relax security rules. Company devices must use full-disk encryption, an automatic screen lock of five minutes or less, and the company VPN when connecting to internal systems from public networks. Working in public places is allowed, but confidential customer data must never be visible on screen in shared spaces. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the security team within one hour, as described in the incident response guide.
